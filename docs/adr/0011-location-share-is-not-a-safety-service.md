# 0011. Location share is the senior's choice and not a safety service

Status: Accepted (2026-10-26). Not yet implemented; the Family page keeps its placeholder card.

The senior turns location sharing on and off on their own screen, and it is off by default. Their
family group sees only the latest approximate area and the time it was shared ("Near Toa Payoh
Central · 10:42 am"), taken while the Care+ page is open on the senior's device. No history is kept,
and there are no geofences, "left home" warnings or other alerts. The screen says plainly that Care+
is not a safety or missing-person service.

Amended 2026-10-26: the family's view may also show that latest share as a rough circle (about
500 m) on a small map, beside the area name and time. The map never follows the senior and draws no
trail; every rule above still holds. The team's word "tracking" is avoided for this feature.

We know this will not help a senior with dementia who goes missing without having turned sharing
on. We still chose it over always-on or caregiver-enabled tracking because finding a missing person
is emergency monitoring, which the product boundary rules out alongside fall detection and emergency
dispatch, and because switching sharing on for someone who may lack the capacity to agree is a legal
decision a screen cannot make. Sharing enabled by a caregiver on someone's behalf is out of scope.
